Profile

Quality isn't a phase.
It's the whole build.

I'm a software test engineer based in India. I spend my days doing the thing most teams postpone until the sprint is nearly over — asking what happens if, and then proving it.

My work covers the full testing stack: writing test plans from ambiguous requirements, automating regression suites so they run on every commit, poking at APIs until they return something they shouldn't, and reporting defects clearly enough that a developer can reproduce them on the first try.

I care about test suites people trust. A flaky suite that everyone ignores is worse than no suite at all — so I'd rather ship fifty reliable cases than five hundred noisy ones.
